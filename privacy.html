Privacy Policy

On Time: Baseball Eye Trainer · Effective September 29, 2026

The short version

On Time has no accounts and no servers. Your training data stays on your iPhone. The free version shows ads from Google, and Google collects some device data to serve them.

Data that stays on your device

Your sessions, reps, settings and Bat Check results are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them, and Setup › Erase all data deletes them at any time.

Advertising

If you do not subscribe to Pro, the app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on screens outside the drills. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. The app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

On Time is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
